Simulation model

How much CO2 does keeping batteries in the loop save?

A simplified system dynamics model of circular battery production: it links the market diffusion of battery electric vehicles with the resulting battery and lithium demand, the recovery of material from end-of-life batteries, their continued use in 2nd-life applications and the resulting greenhouse gas emissions of battery production.
